Payment Frictions and Financial Integration: The Federal Reserve’s Par Collection System

Abstract

This paper examines the role of payment frictions in financial integration in the United States, focusing on the Federal Reserve’s par collection system. Using historical bank balance sheets and county-level data, I examine banks’ transaction-fee decisions and their relationship with regional deposit patterns. The analysis employs logistic regression and causal-inference methods to investigate how differences in bank fees may have shaped banking activity and the geographic distribution of deposits.
