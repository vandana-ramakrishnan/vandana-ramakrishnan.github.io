Inflation Expectations Anchoring and the State-Dependent Phillips Curve

Overview

I investigate whether the slope of the Phillips curve depends on the anchoring of inflation expectations. Using anchoring indices constructed from the Survey of Professional Forecasters and University of Michigan surveys in an interacted VAR with Bayesian methods, I find that the Phillips curve steepens when expectations become less anchored, amplifying the inflation response to macroeconomic shocks. The findings are robust across alternative specifications, with supporting evidence from selected international economies. The results highlight the role of expectations anchoring in shaping inflation dynamics and macroeconomic stability.
