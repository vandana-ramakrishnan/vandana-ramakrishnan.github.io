Welcome to my website!

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Economics at the University of California, Irvine. My research interests are in empirical macroeconomics, monetary economics, macro-finance, and economic history. I study how economic news and policy announcements shape household inflation expectations and inflation pricing in financial markets, and how expectations anchoring influences inflation dynamics.

My research experience includes a Ph.D. fellowship at the Federal Reserve Bank of New York and earlier research positions at the Reserve Bank of India, the National Institute of Public Finance and Policy, and the National Stock Exchange of India.
