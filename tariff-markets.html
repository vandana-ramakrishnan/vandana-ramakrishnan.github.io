Tariff Attention and Inflation Expectations: Evidence from Financial Markets

Vandana Ramakrishnan and Farah Tohme

Overview

We study how tariff-news attention affects market-based inflation compensation using a novel tariff-attention index constructed from Bloomberg news data and daily local projections of TIPS breakevens and inflation swaps across maturities. We find contrasting market responses across the two U.S. trade-war episodes. During 2018–2021, both measures decline following increased tariff attention, consistent with a growth-negative channel. In 2025, inflation swaps rise while short-maturity TIPS breakevens decline, highlighting the importance of accounting for instrument-specific factors, particularly TIPS liquidity, when extracting inflation expectations from market-based inflation compensation.
